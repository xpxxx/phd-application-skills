# Final quality gates

Apply these gates after drafting and again after any humanization or formatting pass.

## Evidence gate

- Every consequential date, number, credential, role, output, result, and publication state maps to a fact ID or decision ID.
- No `conflicting`, prohibited, or unresolved claim appears as fact.
- `User-stated` claims are within their approved wording and are not presented as independently verified.
- Personal contribution is distinguishable from team or project outcomes.
- Completed work, work in progress, unpublished work, forthcoming work, and proposed future work remain distinct.
- No new fact or stronger claim entered during stylistic revision.

## Target gate

- The document answers the requested genre and all binding instructions.
- Names, institution, project title, direction, deadline references, and addressee are current and correct.
- The fit argument relies on verified target details, not generic prestige language.
- Required disclosures and requested choices are included.
- The document respects page, word, file, language, and formatting limits.

## Applicant-control gate

- The applicant approved the central positioning and emphasis.
- Personal motivations and future goals reflect the applicant's own confirmation.
- Sensitive chronology or weakness explanations use the applicant-approved factual account.
- Intentionally omitted unresolved items have not reappeared through paraphrase.
- No placeholder remains in a document labeled final.

## Voice and humanization gate

- Generic AI phrasing, inflated significance, empty intensifiers, repetitive transitions, and clause-stacked sentences have been removed where appropriate.
- The document still sounds appropriate for its genre: compact for a CV, personal and specific for a letter, precise and research-led for an RP.
- Humanization did not alter numbers, citations, names, chronology, qualifications, or publication status.
- The voice was learned from authentic writing samples only as style, not as evidence.
- Any AI-use disclosure accurately describes the real workflow.

## Delivery gate

- The clean document contains no internal fact IDs, evidence labels, review comments, or hidden instructions.
- A sidecar claim map and concise change report remain available for audit.
- Source files were not overwritten.
- The Overleaf-compatible LaTeX source is synchronized with the delivered version, and internal audit files are not present inside the uploadable project.
- The LaTeX project compiles with its intended engine; missing references, overfull boxes, clipped content, blank pages, and page-limit violations were checked.
- The compiled PDF was rendered and every page was visually checked with the appropriate PDF workflow.

Do not call the document final if any gate fails. Report the failed gate and the exact confirmation, evidence, or edit needed.
