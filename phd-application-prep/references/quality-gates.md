# Quality gates for application-preparation handoff

Read this reference before declaring an evidence base, target brief, or evidence handoff ready.

## Evidence integrity

- Every consequential number, date, credential, publication status, and performance claim has a locator.
- Self-authored materials are not treated as independent verification.
- Team outcomes and personal contributions are separate.
- Proposed methods and future interests are not represented as completed research.
- `Forthcoming`, `under review`, `in preparation`, and `published` are not conflated.
- Conflicting facts remain visible until resolved.
- Grade conversions and rankings use institution-backed evidence or are labeled calculations.

## Target integrity

- Eligibility, funding, deadlines, and required documents come from current authoritative sources.
- The brief distinguishes requirements from preferences and interpretation.
- Supervisor fit uses specific current research evidence, not keyword overlap alone.
- Access dates are recorded for mutable web information.

## Narrative integrity

- The primary positioning is supported by multiple concrete experiences.
- Weaknesses are neither hidden through misleading wording nor overemphasized.
- Gaps are handled through omission, verification, or a concise factual explanation.
- Each recommended narrative route is plausible for the target and distinct from the others.
- The handoff preserves relevant complexity instead of inventing a seamless life story.

## Operational integrity

- Source files are untouched.
- New artifacts have clear dates or versions.
- Sensitive data is included only when necessary.
- Open questions are specific, prioritized, and answerable.
- A later writer can trace every approved factual claim back to the fact bank.
- The artifacts contain no submission-ready prose or stylistic rewriting.

## Readiness labels

- **Ready:** no unresolved blocking issue; approved evidence supports the intended document.
- **Ready with caveats:** drafting may proceed, but named claims must remain qualified or omitted.
- **Not ready:** eligibility, identity, timeline, attribution, or central evidence has an unresolved conflict.

Report the label and the reasons. Do not call an artifact ready merely because it is complete in format.
